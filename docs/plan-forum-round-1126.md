## Stage status

Each stage records here what was done, with its decision's source.

**R6: done (26.11.2026; Fabio Emmolo, points 1 and 2).** *Part values as fields.* A placed part's published parameters, derived ones too, are element fields named by their keys, worked out on every ask from the part's table and the object's own values (nothing stored): a platform hook, `DerivedFields.AddNamed` (fields whose names an element's own definition gives, `NamedField`), read by `ParamFormulas.Read` (formulas, schedule columns, filters) after the user's own parameters and before attributes and quantities, by `TagText.Value` (tags and live text, a length in the project's unit), and listed by `FieldCatalog.Across` and `Available` among *Its parameters* in the set `Lapin_PartParameters` (`Lapin_PartParameters.L` when another field has the name); Studio adds them for its classes (`Lapin.Studio/PartFields.cs`). An unpublished parameter is no field. *Part parameters as quantities.* A parameter may carry `measure: length | count | area | volume` (absent: none, as before; refused on a text, a yes/no or an angle, dropped when the kind becomes one; `updatePartParam {changes: {measure}}`, null or `none` clears; shown in `partTree`). A placed part with marked parameters gives the set `AuthoredQuantities.Set` = `Lapin_PartQuantities` (Platform, `Module.cs`) beside its base quantities, each quantity named by its key: schedules read it (`Lapin_PartQuantities.L`), IFC export writes it as that `IfcElementQuantity` (ids stable), and the estimate's articles on the whole element read it first (`Measures.Quantity`, `Parts.Select`: an article by length on a fence of L = 20 m gives 20 m; a count is the marked value, not one per element; a measure the part did not mark reads the body as before). No `IProjectUpgrade`: nothing stored changes, a part without marks measures and exports as before. Interface: the parameter table's **Measure as** column (`measureOptions`, `measureChanges` in `partParamModel.ts`), three new strings in every language. Tests `PartFieldsTests` (3: schedule columns L, derived N, a formula and a text, filters, tags, the pickers; an article per metre and per piece from the marks, defaults unchanged, cleared and undone, refusals; IFC set, stable ids, a package reopened unchanged); `partParamModel.test.ts` grown. Guide: `docs/user/studio-parts.md` (*A part's values in schedules, filters and the estimate*), `schedules.md`, `cost.md` and their Romanian pages. Left: the planning module's productivity norms do not read the marked measures yet (they measure by the element's own quantities); the `studio-part-params` picture needs retaking to show the new column; an article basis by a lowercase key (`h`) is refused by the articles' basis check, so a second marked parameter of the same measure is named by an uppercase key.
