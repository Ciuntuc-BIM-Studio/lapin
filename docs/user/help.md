## Ask Me

Click **Ask Me**, the rabbit at the top right, or press <kbd>F1</kbd> anywhere. Type your question in your own words, in English or Romanian. Ask Me answers from the questions users have asked before, and a button takes you straight to the right place in the app.

![Ask Me answering how to copy a wall](shots/ask-me.webp)

If Ask Me has no answer, **Send the question** sends it to us. We answer it, and the answer is added to Ask Me for everyone.

## The help menu

The **?** button next to Ask Me opens the help menu:

![The help menu](shots/help-menu.webp)

- **Ask Lapin…** opens Ask Me.
- **User guide** opens this guide.
- **Report an issue…** tells us what went wrong, or sends an idea or a question. Say what happened and what you expected, and add a screenshot if it helps. The app's version and its last error messages go with the report.
- **Community (Skool)** is where users and the team talk.
- **Check for updates…** is in the desktop app only.
- **About Lapin…** shows the version, the licence, a link to the source code, and what helpware means.

  ![About Lapin](shots/about.webp)
- **English · Română · Español · Deutsch · Français · Dansk** chooses the interface's language. Your choice is remembered on this computer. This guide is in English and Romanian: with the interface in another language, **User guide** opens it in English.

## Updates

The desktop app looks for a newer version when it starts, and asks before it installs one. You can also check with **? → Check for updates…**. The web version is always the latest.
